Why no two are alike
Glaze pools and breaks differently on every curve. Expect variation in tone, small iron spots and a soft crawl near the seam. Photos show a recent firing, not your exact jar.
Care
Watertight and safe for fresh flowers. Wipe with a damp cloth. Avoid the dishwasher; the unglazed foot ring can stain.
Returns

Three weeks from wet clay to your door

  1. Day 1

    Thrown in halves

    Two bowls are thrown on the wheel and left to firm up overnight.

  2. Day 2

    Joined and trimmed

    The halves are scored, joined at the rim and paddled into one round form.

  3. Day 10

    Bisque fired

    Once bone dry, the jar is fired to 900°C so it can take glaze.

  4. Day 18

    Glaze fired

    Dipped in your chosen glaze and fired to 1280°C in a gas kiln for 11 hours.
